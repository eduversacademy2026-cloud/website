4. Data Sharing & Disclosure

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information. We share data only in these limited situations:

4.1 Google (Form Data Storage)

When you submit our contact form, your name, phone, email, and timestamp are transmitted securely to Google Sheets via a Google Apps Script web application. Google acts as a data processor on our behalf. The Sheet is private and accessible only to authorised Eduverse Academy team members. Governed by Google's Privacy Policy.

4.2 Cloudinary (Image Hosting)

Team member and course images are hosted on Cloudinary. Cloudinary does not receive or process any personal data you submit through our forms.

4.3 Vercel (Website Hosting)

Our website is hosted on Vercel, which may process standard server request data (IP, user-agent) as part of normal web hosting. Governed by Vercel's Privacy Policy.

4.4 Legal Requirements

We may disclose data if required by Indian law, a court order, or a government authority, or to protect the rights, property, or safety of Eduverse Academy, our students, or others.

Summary: Your personal data is shared with Google (form storage) only. It is not shared with any advertiser, social media platform, data broker, or other third party.

5. Data Security

We protect your information using the following measures:

  • HTTPS Encryption — all data is encrypted using TLS/SSL in transit.
  • Encrypted Transmission to Google — form data is sent to Google over encrypted HTTPS.
  • Restricted Access — the Google Sheet is private, accessible only to authorised team members.
  • No Third-Party Trackers — no advertising scripts or tracking pixels that could expose your data.

While we implement industry-standard measures, no system is 100% secure. If you believe your data has been compromised, contact us at eduversacademy2026@gmail.com immediately.

Eduverse Academy provides educational services to students including children under the age of 13.

Important: We do not knowingly collect personal information directly from children under 13. Our enquiry forms must be completed by a parent, guardian, or adult student (18 years or older) on behalf of the child.

  • If you are a parent or guardian and believe we have inadvertently collected personal information from a child under 13 without your consent, please contact us immediately at eduversacademy2026@gmail.com. We will delete it within 7 days.
